Leadership strategic și dezvoltarea păcii durabile

15–17 mai 2025Chișinău

Programul detaliat este publicat în limba engleză.

This intensive program is designed to empower current and aspiring young leaders with the tools and knowledge necessary to drive sustainable change within their organizations and communities. Participants will explore key concepts of strategic leadership while focusing on sustainable development principles, including environmental stewardship, social equity, and economic viability.

Key Focus Areas: International Institutions and Development Strategies; SDGs and Sustainability Policy; Social Cohesion for Community Growth; Community Engagement Strategies; Leadership and Public Diplomacy; Development and Gender; Food Sustainability and Security; Sustainable Energy and the Green Agenda.
