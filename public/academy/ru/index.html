ABOUT US

The INSPIRE Network (Inter-University Network for Sustainable Peace, Innovation, Research, and Education) — collaborative initiative was launched in the Republic of Moldova, bringing together universities and NGOs.

In 2025, the INSPIRE Academy of Future Leaders will host three specialized schools, each designed to equip young leaders with the knowledge and skills to address global challenges and drive meaningful change in their communities. Previous experience could be found at https://peace.education.md

INSPIRE Network established Centers for Civic and Global Engagement within participating universities. They serve as local hubs for collaboration, offering resources and support for students and young researchers.

InspireEdu (Inspiring Education for Future Leaders) – is a project funding by the Embassy of the Kingdom of the Netherlands in Moldova

Values & Core Areas

The INSPIRE Network aims to be a platform for training leaders capable of contributing to sustainable development, shaping global and regional political and social change, fostering international cooperation, and strengthening ties between academic communities.

Tech Peacemakers: Digital Solutions for Social Cohesion

This intensive course equips emerging leaders with the skills to harness technology for positive societal impact. Participants will explore cutting-edge tools like AI, blockchain, and data analytics while addressing the ethical and social dimensions of technology.
Rooted in peacebuilding and social cohesion, the program emphasizes how digital innovation can bridge divides, foster inclusion, and strengthen trust in communities. Key topics include cybersecurity, responsible tech use, and combating misinformation.
 
Key Focus Areas: Emerging Technologies and Leadership in Tech; Digital Transformation and Innovation Management; Cybersecurity Awareness and Sustainability in Tech; Diversity and Inclusion in Tech; Future Skills and Workforce Development; Mentorship and Coaching.

Future Voices: Media and Communication for Young Innovators

This intensive program is designed to equip young innovators with essential communication skills to effectively convey their ideas in a rapidly changing media landscape. Participants will explore key topics such as storytelling techniques, public speaking, and digital literacy, empowering them to engage diverse audiences across various platforms. The program emphasizes ethical media practices, visual communication, and personal branding while also covering conflict resolution, cultural awareness, and leadership for social cohesion.
 
Key Focus Areas: Understanding Communication Models; Public Speaking and Presentation Skills; Media Ethics and Responsibility; Leveraging Social Media for Innovation; Visual Communication; Building a Personal Brand; Conflict Resolution and Mediation; Building Trust and Relationships; Cultural Awareness and Inclusivity.

Who Should Attend the Program?

The INSPIRE Academy of Future Leaders is designed for:

Aspiring changemakers

18-40 years old eager to drive impact in their communities and beyond

Students & young professionals

passionate about leadership, diplomacy, and global challenges

Future innovators & problem-solvers

committed to sustainable development and international

Researchers & activists

looking to deepen their expertise and network with like-minded peers

Language Requirements

The School is conducted in Russian, Romanian, and English with translation provided. Proficiency in at least one of these languages is required

School Selection

Applicants can apply for all three Schools. However, only the 10 most active participants will have the opportunity to attend all three programs in 2025

GRANT COMPETITION

As part of the INSPIRE Academy 2025 program, implemented with the support of the Embassy of the Kingdom of the Netherlands in the Republic of Moldova, a mini-grant competition for students participating in the three INSPIRE schools is planned. The initiative aims to promote youth leadership, civic engagement, and the values of social cohesion. The competition will enable students from Comrat, Tiraspol, and Chișinău to implement their own ideas — from educational and technological projects to cultural and media initiatives — fostering collaboration and connection among young people
