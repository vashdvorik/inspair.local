About us

The INSPIRE Network (Inter-University Network for Sustainable Peace, Innovation, Research, and Education) is a collaborative initiative launched in the Republic of Moldova, bringing together universities and NGOs.

In 2025 the INSPIRE Academy of Future Leaders hosts three specialised schools, each designed to equip young leaders with the knowledge and skills to address global challenges and drive change in their communities. Earlier experience: peace.education.md.

The network established Centers for Civic and Global Engagement within participating universities. They serve as local hubs for collaboration, offering resources and support for students and young researchers.

InspireEdu (Inspiring Education for Future Leaders) is a project funded by the Embassy of the Kingdom of the Netherlands in Moldova.

Language requirements

The schools are conducted in Russian, Romanian and English with translation provided. Proficiency in at least one of these languages is required.

School selection

Applicants can apply for all three schools. Only the 10 most active participants will attend all three programmes in 2025.

Grant competition

As part of INSPIRE Academy 2025, implemented with the support of the Embassy of the Kingdom of the Netherlands in the Republic of Moldova, a mini-grant competition is planned for students of the three schools. It promotes youth leadership, civic engagement and the values of social cohesion, enabling students from Comrat, Tiraspol and Chișinău to implement their own ideas — from educational and technological projects to cultural and media initiatives.
